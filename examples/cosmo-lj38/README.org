* Source

Course: Statistical methods in atomistic computer simulations
(Ceriotti, EPFL COSMO).
https://www.epfl.ch/labs/cosmo/index-html/teaching/page-110059-en-html/

Materials: =sampling_atomistic_simulations-exercises.zip= (2019-11).
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0.

=ts.all= is the 10-D coordination-count trajectory from exercise 5
(counts n4..n13, columns 3-12). =lj38.xyz= is the fcc truncated
octahedron start. =lj38.17.xyz= / =lj38.19.xyz= are metadynamics
snapshots. =lj38_fcc.xyz= / =lj38_ico.xyz= are the two competing
minima.

The public zip and =lab-cosmo/sampling-tutorial= ship =ts.all= (the
exercise 5.5 transition-state ensemble, 3451 frames). They do not ship
=out.all=, the long MD used for the JCTC 2013 landscape
(Ceriotti, Tribello, Parrinello, DOI 10.1021/ct3010563). The composed
figure is therefore a Gaussian KDE of the TSE embedding, drawn in that
paper's panel class (connected F/eps blob, colorbar 0-2, structures
on the map with leader lines, integer CN 3-12 bars). It is not a
reconstruction of the missing =out.all= sample.

Exercise 5 parameters (verbatim):

- high-D sigmoid =5,8,1=
- low-D sigmoid =5,2,2=
- =kT* = 0.168=
- FES grid about =[-15,15]^2=

=run.sh= auto-pads the TSE embedding. To force the course box:

#+begin_src sh
landfold fes --input out/ts.proj --nx 80 --ny 80 --kt 0.168 \
    --xmin -15 --xmax 15 --ymin -15 --ymax 15 \
    --csv out/fes.csv --svg out/fes.svg
#+end_src

* Run

#+begin_src sh
# remote builder, release landfold on PATH
./run.sh
# compose_fes.py writes out/lj38_fes.png and lj38_fes.png
#+end_src
