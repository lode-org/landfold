=ts.all= is the 10-D coordination-count trajectory from exercise 5
(counts n4..n13, columns 3-12). =lj38.xyz= is the fcc truncated
octahedron start. =lj38.17.xyz= / =lj38.19.xyz= are metadynamics
snapshots. =lj38_fcc.xyz= / =lj38_ico.xyz= are the two competing
minima.

Exercise 5 parameters (verbatim):

- high-D sigmoid =5,8,1=
- low-D sigmoid =5,2,2=
- =kT* = 0.168=
- FES grid about =[-15,15]^2=
