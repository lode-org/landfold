* Transfer

Generalised sigmoid (Ceriotti 2011, DOI 10.1073/pnas.1108486108):

=F(x) = 1 - (1 + (2^{a/b}-1)(x/σ)^a )^{-b/a}=

equals 1/2 at =x = σ=.

* Stress

χ = (1/Z) Σ_{i<j} w_ij [ (1-imix)(F_HD(D_ij)-F_LD(d_ij))^2 + imix (D_ij-d_ij)^2 ]

* Solvers

| Arm | Method | Citation |
|-----+--------+----------|
| Standard (default) | Polak-Ribiere CG + Brent | Polak 1969; Brent 1973 |
| Stochastic | random pair mini-batches | Robbins and Monro 1951 |
| MDS init | Torgerson double-centre | Torgerson 1952 |

* FES

=F/kT = -ln(ρ/ρ_max)= on a 2-D histogram of the embedding.
